Bamboo setup intake

Fill this out, download the completed JSON, then upload that file to your setup operator with the repository.

Works locally in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server. Do not enter passwords, API keys, access tokens, database connection strings or employee data. Unresolved provider choices may stay deferred. Setup choices do not activate services or authorize purchases.

Required fields are marked *. Examples are illustrative: replace sample names and example.com with your own details. Leave optional unknowns blank. About 10 minutes. You can save a draft and import it later.
